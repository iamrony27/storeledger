Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Application: Store Ledger (com.gwd.book) • Version: 2.0.0

shield 1. Core Philosophy: Zero Surveillance & Offline-First

Store Ledger was engineered with a strict offline-first and zero-surveillance architecture. We believe that small business owners, shopkeepers, and independent contractors should have complete sovereignty over their financial records.

Summary: Store Ledger does not collect, transmit, sell, monetize, or profile your personal data or your customers' financial balances. All data lives on your device unless you explicitly configure your own private Supabase backend.

database 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All information you input into Store Ledger is stored strictly inside an isolated local database (SQLite) directly on your physical hardware:

  • Customer Profiles: Customer names, phone numbers, notes, and avatars.
  • Financial Transactions: Credit/Debit amounts, dates, bill slips, reference tags, and settlement statuses.
  • App Preferences: Selected currency symbol (INR, USD, EUR, etc.), decimal toggle preference, and theme settings.

This data never leaves your device under any circumstances during standard offline operation. The app requires no phone verification, no sign-in, and no registration to function.

cloud_sync 3. Optional Bring-Your-Own-Cloud (Supabase Sync)

Store Ledger offers an optional cloud backup and multi-device synchronization engine powered by Supabase:

  • Zero Intermediary Servers: Store Ledger does not operate any centralized servers. When you enable Cloud Sync, your device communicates directly with your personal Supabase project URL via encrypted TLS 1.3 connections.
  • Your Private Credentials: Your Supabase Project URL and Anon Key are stored locally in Android Encrypted SharedPreferences / Keychain. We never have access to your keys or database.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): The provided SQL schema enforces PostgreSQL Row Level Security, ensuring only authenticated users can read or write their own business ledger records.
  • Safe Disconnection: Disconnecting Supabase at any time wipes the credentials from your local device immediately while keeping your remote data fully intact.

key 4. Android Device Permissions Explained

Store Ledger requests minimal Android permissions only when explicitly needed to perform user-initiated tasks:

  • Storage / Media Access (Scoped Storage): Required solely to save exported PDF statement reports and encrypted JSON backup snapshots to your device's Downloads or Documents directory.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively to communicate with your self-hosted Supabase instance when you manually activate Cloud Sync. In offline mode, network capabilities are dormant.
  • Phone / WhatsApp Intent: When you tap "Call" or "WhatsApp" on a customer's statement, Store Ledger launches your system dialer or the official WhatsApp application via Android Intents. Store Ledger does not listen to calls or read incoming messages.

block 5. Third-Party Trackers & Advertising

Store Ledger contains:

  • NO Advertising SDKs: No Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, or any other marketing trackers.
  • NO Analytics Trackers: No Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or AppsFlyer telemetry.
  • NO Crash Telemetry: Crash logs are not automatically uploaded to third-party endpoints.

lock 6. Data Security & Integrity

Store Ledger employs enterprise-grade cryptographic controls to safeguard data integrity:

  • Exact Integer Arithmetic: All financial calculations are evaluated in exact minor units (e.g. paise/cents) to eliminate floating-point rounding discrepancies.
  • SHA-256 Verified Backups: Manual backup archives are checksummed with SHA-256 to ensure zero file corruption during transit or storage.

child_care 7. Children’s Privacy

Store Ledger is a business accounting tool designed for commercial enterprises and independent professionals. We do not knowingly collect or solicit information from children under the age of 13 in compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
